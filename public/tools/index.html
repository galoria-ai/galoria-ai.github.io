Where should I start?

Make a copy of important photos, choose one folder, and separate obvious groups such as screenshots, camera photos, scans, and design assets. Review ambiguous images individually. Organizing means choosing useful destinations; it does not require deleting anything.

When a reviewed organizer helps

Manual grouping works for a small folder. For a larger mixed image folder on Windows 10 or 11, Galoria analyzes supported images locally and proposes destinations for you to review before moving them. It can still misclassify ambiguous images, so reassign or exclude those items. It does not delete photos or detect duplicates.
