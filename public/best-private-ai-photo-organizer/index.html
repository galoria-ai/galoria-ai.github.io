Private photo organization needs more than a no-cloud slogan

A private photo organizer should explain what happens on the device, which files and signals it supports, and whether the proposed moves remain editable. Privacy is useful only when the workflow also protects against wrong categories and accidental moves.

Galoria combines local image analysis with a visible plan. You can reassign ambiguous photos, exclude items, rename destination folders, and keep a local history for the latest-operation undo workflow.

Step-by-step workflow

  1. Choose a copied Pictures, Screenshots, Downloads, or camera-roll folder.
  2. Let Galoria analyze supported image files locally.
  3. Review categories, destination names, exclusions, and conflicts.
  4. Apply only the approved moves and keep the local history available for undo.

Frequently asked questions

Does Galoria upload my photos?

Galoria is designed around local image analysis and planning, so photos do not need to be uploaded to a cloud organizer for the organization workflow.

Can I review photo categories before moving anything?

Yes. Galoria shows proposed categories and destinations, and lets you reassign or exclude images before applying changes.

Which images can Galoria organize?

Galoria supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, BMP, TIFF, HEIC, and HEIF files, subject to the workflow's local analysis capabilities.
